ABOUT MILGAYA

Making lost & found simpler.

Milgaya is designed to make returning lost belongings easier. A small QR code can connect a finder with the owner without publicly displaying the owner's personal information.

Privacy First

Your personal contact details don't need to be displayed publicly.

Simple

Scan the QR and follow the simple process.

Everyday Use

Use it on bags, wallets, laptops, keys and other belongings.
